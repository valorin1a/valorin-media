## LinkedIn (carousel as PDF, document title "The CFO Week, 20 Nov 2026")
Four things on a finance desk this week, in plain words.

1. Tax: the Federal Tax Authority lists 30 Nov 2026 as the final VAT filing date. Check which period it covers for you.
2. Loans: if your loan is variable-rate, ask your bank how a rate change would move your monthly payment.
3. E-invoicing: larger businesses start on 1 Jan 2027 (Ministerial Resolution 66 of 2026, AED 50m+). Smaller businesses have a later timeline.
4. Cash: if your biggest customer paid 30 days late, could you still cover salaries? It is a question, not a claim about any company.
