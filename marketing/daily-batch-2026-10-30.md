Instagram:
What is DSCR, and why does your bank ask for it?

Debt service coverage ratio = cash available for debt service ÷ loan principal and interest due over the same period.
Illustrative example (not a real company, AED thousands): 1,500 ÷ 1,200 = 1.25x.

Below 1.0x, the cash the business generates doesn't cover its repayments.
Each lender sets its own minimum and definition, usually in the facility letter.
Test it 12 months ahead before you borrow more.
