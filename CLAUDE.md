# Valorin Peppol Pulse – workflow for Claude

Public repository: holds only finished, public social media files and the tools that make them. Never commit
credentials, account IDs, client data or anything private. Account IDs live in the private Google Doc
"Valorin Marketing Playbook (read first)" in the user's Drive folder "Valorin Peppol Pulse" – read it before posting.

## Each update (user sends only the Ionite UAE number)
1. `cd tools && npm install` (first time in a workspace). Chromium headless shell:
   `/opt/pw-browsers/chromium_headless_shell-1194/chrome-linux/headless_shell` (path is set in render.js / video.js;
   adjust if the workspace differs). ffmpeg with libx264 is required for the video.
2. Previous reading = last line of `data/pulse.csv`. Write `tools/data.json.local`:
   `{"date":"YYYY-MM-DD","registered":N,"previousDate":"YYYY-MM-DD","previousRegistered":N,"totalCompanies":1400000}`
   Voiceover (same voice as the Valorin Marketing studio, for brand consistency): `pip install --break-system-packages kokoro-onnx soundfile`,
   then download the Kokoro-82M files (Apache 2.0) into /tmp/kokoro, as in `marketing/studio/setup.sh`.
   Add `"voiceModel":"kokoro:/tmp/kokoro","voiceSpeaker":"bm_george@0.85"` to data.json.local (voice George, speed 0.85, en-gb).
   The older Piper LibriTTS option still works (`"voiceModel":"<dir>/en-us-libritts-high.onnx","voiceSpeaker":92`) but is no longer the default.
   Music is generated by tools/audio.py (original, no third-party music). Audio is normalised to about -14 LUFS.
3. `node render.js data.json.local ../media/YYYY-MM-DD/card.png` and
   `node video.js data.json.local ../media/YYYY-MM-DD/video.mp4` (create the folder first). Look at the card before use.
   If two updates fall on one day, use folder `YYYY-MM-DD-2`.
4. Append the reading to `data/pulse.csv` with the `angle` and `opening` columns filled in, commit as Claude <noreply@anthropic.com>, push to `main`.
5. Media URLs for Metricool:
   `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/valorin1a/valorin-media/main/media/<folder>/card.png` and `.../video.mp4`.
6. Schedule in Metricool at the times in "Posting times" below (one post per network, own caption): card → Instagram, Facebook, Google Business Profile
   (type publication), Pinterest (board "UAE E-Invoicing Tracker", link https://www.valorin.ae); video → YouTube
   (type short, madeForKids false, category EDUCATION) and TikTok (commercialContentOwnBrand true).
   YouTube description must end with: "Voiceover: synthetic voice (Kokoro-82M TTS, Apache 2.0). Music: original."
   Video length follows the voiceover (about 50 s at speed 0.85).
7. Append a row to the Google Sheet "Peppol Pulse Log". Give the user LinkedIn and X captions.

## Rules (summary – full rules in the Drive playbook)
- Sourced, dated numbers only. "Listed in the Peppol Directory", not "registered". "1.4M+" UAE companies.
- Penalties per Cabinet Decision No. 106 of 2025, "for in-scope businesses". "General information only, not legal or tax advice."
- FTA / MoF hashtags allowed as topic tags only (#FTA #FederalTaxAuthority #UAEMinistryOfFinance #MoFUAE); never @mention
  government accounts or imply endorsement; add "Valorin is an independent advisory firm, not affiliated with the FTA or MoF."

## Daily wording (every day different)
- Choose an angle that is not in the last two rows of the `angle` column in `data/pulse.csv`. Angles:
  1. Milestone: a round number, or the "1 in 100" comparison.
  2. Movement: what changed since the last reading.
  3. Deadline: state the date. Use a day count only when posting the same day.
  4. Practical step: one action a finance team can take this week.
  5. Myth versus fact: a common e-invoicing misconception, corrected.
  6. Question to the reader: a question that invites a reply.
  7. Behind the number: what the directory count does and does not show.
  8. Penalty check: who it applies to, with the disclaimer.
- The opening line must differ from the `opening` column of the last seven rows.
- Vary sentence length. Avoid stock phrases ("Exciting news", "Don't miss out", "Stay ahead") and all capitals.
- Every caption keeps the fixed items: the figure with its source and date, Cabinet Decision No. 106 of 2025 for penalties, "General information only, not legal or tax advice", the independent-firm line, the FTA/MoF topic tags (not on Google Business Profile), both brand lines, and the contact line.
